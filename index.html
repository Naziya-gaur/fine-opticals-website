About Fine Optical

Established in 1993, Fine Optical is a trusted manufacturer of precision optical components serving scientific, industrial, medical, and commercial applications. With decades of expertise, we specialize in producing high-accuracy lenses and optical elements that meet strict performance and quality standards.

Manufacturer of precision optical lenses and custom optics for microscopes, laser instruments, telescopes, medical and scientific instruments. Specialized in Plano Convex, Plano Concave, Bi-Convex, Bi-Concave, Achromatic doublet lens, Optical window, Meniscus lenses in N-BK7, Fused Silica and other optical glass materials. Providing custom optical components with polishing, grinding and precision finishing as per customer drawings and specifications.
